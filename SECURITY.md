# Security

OpenFill handles your AI service keys. It stores them in Photoshop's secure storage on your computer and sends them only to the service you pick (`api.openai.com` or `generativelanguage.googleapis.com`).

If you find a way OpenFill could leak a key, send data somewhere else, or harm a user's files, please **report it privately**: use [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/damanjz/openfill/security/advisories/new) on this repository. Don't open a public issue.

You'll get a reply within a week. Fixes for confirmed problems ship as a new release with credit to you, unless you'd rather stay anonymous.
